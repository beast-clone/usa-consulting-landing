Before you enrol

Ask any consultant these — including us.

USMLE-to-Match is a multi-year commitment. These are the questions worth asking before you commit to a program.

Who is the US physician mentor, exactly?

Ask for their background before you start. A named mentor with US practice experience is a different thing from a general “expert panel.”

What happens in the monthly progress check?

Ask what gets reviewed, what changes if you fall behind schedule, and who adjusts the plan.

Who finds the USCE placements?

Ask how observerships and externships are identified, and whether the fit is based on your actual profile and goals or a generic list.

Can you guarantee a Match?

No consultant can. What a program can do is make sure your application, your USCE record and your interview prep give a program nothing to reject.

Questions

Answered honestly, including when the answer is “it depends”.

What exactly is the USA Standard Consulting Program?

A structured program covering USMLE timeline planning, exam registration, ECFMG profile setup, USCE and clinical rotation matching, LOR strategy, portfolio building, CV writing, one-on-one mentoring with a US physician, monthly progress checks, and ERAS and residency Match guidance through interviews and follow-up.

Do I need to have started USMLE already?

No. The program starts with timeline and preparation planning, so it works whether you’re still in MBBS, just graduated, or already partway through Step 1 or Step 2 CK. Tell us your stage on the form and we’ll tell you what the program covers from there.

What does the “mentorship by a US physician” actually cover?

Two distinct points in the journey: Step 1, Step 2 CK and Step 3 preparation — test strategy and US healthcare system insights — and later, ERAS strategy — program selection and how to stand out in your application.

Who finds my clinical rotations or observerships?

We help identify suitable hospitals, clinics and programs, and support finding observerships and externships based on your preferences, plus guidance on affiliation applications, documentation and submission timelines.

Can you guarantee I’ll Match?

No. Nobody can guarantee a residency Match — that decision sits with the programs, not with any consultant. What we can do is make sure your USMLE scores, USCE record, application and interview prep give programs nothing to hold against you.

What if my plan needs to change mid-program?

That’s what the individualised monthly progress checks are for — scheduled sessions to review where you are and adjust the strategy, with ongoing, actionable feedback rather than a fixed plan that ignores how your prep is actually going.
